How the model thinks

1

Demand can wobble

Five scenarios are created around each nominal demand value. CV controls how wide that wobble is.

2

Short queues cost less

E[(D−n)⁺] = Σᵥ pᵥ max(v−n,0) measures the average unmet demand at staffing level n.

3

QAE checks the expectation

Probabilities and shortfall are encoded as amplitudes. This app evaluates the noiseless payoff amplitude analytically.

4

A quadratic curve becomes a QUBO

L̂(n)=a₂n²+a₁n+a₀ approximates the exact shortfall curve. Eligible officer decisions become binary variables, with pairwise penalties preventing double-booking.

5

SA samples low-energy plans

The app builds the notebook's explicit QUBO and runs classical simulated annealing with configurable reads, sweeps, and seed. QA and VQE are not executed.
