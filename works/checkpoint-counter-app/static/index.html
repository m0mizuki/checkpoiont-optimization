Method notes

What the model is doing

01

Discrete uncertain demand

Each demand cell becomes five scenarios centered on its nominal value. Spread is proportional to the checkpoint type's coefficient of variation.

02

Expected shortfall

For staffing level n, the risk term is E[(D - n)⁺] = Σᵥ pᵥ max(v - n, 0).

03

QAE payoff encoding

Scenario probabilities are loaded as amplitudes. Controlled rotations place normalized shortfall on an ancilla, so its probability of 1 equals the normalized expectation. The local app evaluates this noiseless statevector amplitude analytically.

04

QUBO surrogate & staffing

A quadratic fit âL(n) = a₂n² + a₁n + a₀ makes the risk curve QUBO-compatible. The app searches feasible counter counts exactly under the same surrogate, then evaluates the chosen plan on the exact loss curve.
